what tonic vault does

tonic vault keeps the patterns and sounds from a po-32 tonic in a library you can name, tag, and play in the browser, using the tonic's own synth engine.

import

start listening, then press write + sound on the tonic. the vault decodes the transfer through the mic or a cable and keeps every new pattern and sound set. sound packs import from their .wav files.

export

pick a pattern for each slot, press acc + sound on the tonic, and send. the backup .wav does the same job from any device that can play it.

share

a pattern saves as a .mid for a daw or as a four-bar .wav. its link carries the pattern and its sounds, so anyone can play it or save it to their own vault.

your data

the vault lives only in this browser. use save and load on the library to back it up or move it to another device.
